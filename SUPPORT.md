# GeoBoost support

For installation, access, billing or supported-round questions, contact [support@geoboost.win](mailto:support@geoboost.win).

Please include:

- game site and mode;
- GeoBoost version;
- Chrome version;
- what you expected to happen;
- what happened instead;
- a screenshot with personal or payment information removed.
